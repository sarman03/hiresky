How HireSky Operates

Three simple elements working in sync to power your live assistant.

01

Audio & Visual Capture

The Python core intercepts speaker sound from the audio driver and captures designated screen regions (like editor windows).

02

Linguistic Synthesis

Audio is transcribed with Whisper, aggregated with OCR code context, and passed to LLMs to formulate answers.

03

Compositor Overlay Delivery

Answers stream in real-time to your Swift/WPF glassmorphic desktop overlay, entirely invisible to others on the call.
